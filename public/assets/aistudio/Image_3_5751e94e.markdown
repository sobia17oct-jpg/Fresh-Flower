## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an editorial, boutique luxury experience tailored specifically for high-tempo floral atelier operations. The interface balances high-end sensory elegance—reminiscent of curated floristry and botanical apothecary journals—with surgical tactile efficiency needed at checkout.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Style:** Warm Editorial Luxury combined with Tactile Human Ergonomics. It steers clear of clinical, sterile SaaS tropes, utilizing soft cream foundations, deep organic sage structures, and delicate muted rose accents.
- **Atmosphere:** Serene, grounded, prestigious, and effortless.
- **Touch Principles:** Built primarily for counter-mounted tablets and touch-screen terminals. Every primary action maintains a minimum 48px to 56px interactive footprint with spatial padding to prevent accidental inputs during busy storefront rushes.

## Colors
The palette evokes botanical stems, dried floral paper, and soft petals.

- **Primary (`#2E4A3E` - Deep Forest Sage):** Anchors operational actions, active checkout buttons, selected category chips, and major navigational landmarks. It communicates reliability, nature, and architectural poise.
- **Secondary (`#D88C9A` - Dusty Muted Rose):** Used selectively for highlights, promotion badges, secondary notifications, active cart counters, and romantic focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#7A9A8B` - Ethereal Sage):** Bridges neutral framing and bold primary green. Applied to subtle icon accents, hover tints, and secondary tags.
- **Neutral (`#222222` - Dark Charcoal):** Yields high-contrast editorial typography without the harshness of pitch black.
- **Canvas & Surface Foundations:**
  - Base Background: `#FAF8F5` (Soft Cream Canvas)
  - Card & Container Fill: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Alabaster)
  - Subtle Dividing Line: `#EFECE6`
  - Input & Keypad Surface: `#F4F0EB`

## Typography
The system employs a high-contrast editorial pairing:

1. **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for client display names, category headers, checkout total labels, receipt headers, and modal titles.
2. **Precision Modern Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powering product catalog names, touch numeric keypads, cart tallies, and operational status chips. Numbers utilize tabular figures (`tnum`) across all pricing and quantity fields for flawless vertical scanning.
3. **Currency Presentation:** All pricing must explicitly lead with `Rs.` followed by a non-breaking space (e.g., `Rs. 4,500`), set in medium or bold sans-serif weight to guarantee fast recognition under high glare.

## Layout & Spacing
Designed for POS terminals (typically 10" to 15" landscapes) and secondary mobile handheld terminals:

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop / Landscape POS (>= 1024px):** 12-column split-screen layout.
  - Left Zone (Columns 1–8): Dynamic product catalog grid and category ribbon (scrollable, 3 or 4 columns of cards).
  - Right Zone (Columns 9–12): Persistent ticket/order ledger fixed panel with instant checkout keypad and summary totals.
- **Tablet Portrait (768px – 1023px):** Collapsible persistent drawer for ticket summary with sticky bottom action trigger.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** Single-column stacked layout with full-bleed bottom sheet order tray.

### Ergonomic Spacing
- All primary touch components require at least `space-md` separation between touch triggers.
- Edge margins preserve screen padding against POS bezel enclosures.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is expressed through delicate botanical surface tiers and micro-diffused ambient drops:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Soft Cream background (`#FAF8F5`) serving as the ground plane.
- **Level 1 (Crisp Surfaces & Cards):** Flat `#FFFFFF` panels finished with subtle 1px border rules (`#EFECE6`). Avoid heavy drop shadows to prevent visual noise during quick browsing.
- **Level 2 (Active Touch & Modals):** Highly diffused warm shadow (`0 8px 30px rgba(46, 74, 62, 0.08)`) with a pristine white background.
- **Level 3 (Floating Checkout & Drawer Trays):** Elevated sticky checkout bar (`0 -4px 24px rgba(34, 34, 32, 0.05)`).

## Shapes
A roundedness value of `2` provides a balance of organic softness and structured modularity:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges):** 0.5rem (8px).
- **Cards, Keypad Buttons & Standard Buttons:** 0.75rem – 1rem (12px – 16px), delivering inviting touch surfaces.
- **Cart Sidebars & Large Sheets:** 1.5rem (24px) for top outer radii.
- **Category Filter Pills:** Full rounded-full (9999px) capsules for fluid swipe recognition.

## Components

### 1. POS Item Cards
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, 14px outer radius, 1px `#EFECE6` perimeter stroke.
- **Content:** Square aspect-ratio image container with inset soft botanical tint, product name in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans`, bloom variant/stem count in muted text, and price clearly displayed in `Rs.` with 16px semibold numeric styling.
- **Touch Target:** Minimum height 180px, width 140px, full-card tap triggers immediate line-item addition.

### 2. Action Buttons & Numerical Keypad
- **Primary Action (Charge / Pay):** Minimum height 56px, filled `#2E4A3E`, text in `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600, 14px radius. Press state dims to `#22382F`.
- **Secondary / Void Action:** Soft Cream surface with `#2E4A3E` border and label.
- **Quick-Tender Cash Buttons:** Muted Rose fill (`#D88C9A` at 15% opacity), text in `#2E4A3E`, facilitating swift single-touch exact cash workflows (e.g., `Rs. 1,000`, `Rs. 5,000`).
- **Numpad Grid:** 64px square keys, centered numerals, zero border, soft cream pressed feedback.

### 3. Order Ledger / Cart Items
- Row height: Minimum 64px for easy thumb scrolling.
- Quantity adjustment: Embedded segmented control with deep sage icons and 44px hit areas (`+` / `-`).
- High-contrast item subtotal formatted with `Rs.` aligned right.

### 4. Category Filter Chips
- Capsule shaped, 44px touch height, horizontally scrollable with rubber-band boundaries.
- **Selected:** Solid `#2E4A3E` background with `#FAF8F5` text.
- **Inactive:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#EAE6DF` outline, `#222222` text.

### 5. Input Fields & Search
- Integrated search bar for stem lookup: 52px height, soft cream fill (`#F4F0EB`), deep forest sage search icon, 12px rounded corners, instant clear `(×)` hit box.
